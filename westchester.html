Westchester FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you serve all of Westchester County?+

Yes, we provide wheelchair-accessible transportation throughout Westchester County, including White Plains, Scarsdale, Mount Vernon, Rye, and surrounding towns.

Can you transport Westchester residents to NYC hospitals?+

Yes. Many of our Westchester riders travel to Manhattan and Bronx medical centers, and we coordinate routing to get you there on time.

Do you offer school transportation in Westchester?+

Yes, we offer daily and monthly school transportation plans for students with mobility needs throughout Westchester County.
