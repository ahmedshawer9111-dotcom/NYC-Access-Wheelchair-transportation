  • Consistent, punctual pickups timed to the school bell
  • Coordination available with parents, caregivers, and school staff

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What areas does Wheelchair Rides NYC serve?+

We provide wheelchair-accessible transportation across all five NYC boroughs, Westchester County, New Rochelle, Yonkers, Long Island (Nassau and Suffolk), and New Jersey.

Do you provide daily school transportation for students who use wheelchairs?+

Yes. We offer daily and monthly school transportation plans with trained drivers and helpers who assist students on and off the vehicle, timed to the school schedule.

Is this an emergency medical service?+

No. We provide non-emergency wheelchair and mobility transportation only. For a medical emergency, always call 911.

Can you help with hospital discharge transportation?+

Yes. Our drivers and helpers assist with safe, timely transport from hospitals and rehab facilities back home or to a care facility, anywhere across our NY and NJ service area.

Are your drivers trained to assist wheelchair passengers?+

Yes. Every driver and helper is trained in safe passenger assistance, wheelchair securement, and respectful, patient-centered care.

How do I pay for my ride?+

Visit our Pay Your Reservation page, or ask your driver about payment options.
